Atakan Aygun

I develop high-order numerical methods for flows with moving boundaries, built to run on GPUs.

I am a PhD candidate and research assistant at Middle East Technical University, working in the Accelerated Multiphysics Research Group with Dr. Ali Karakus and co-advisor Dr. Tim Warburton. From October 2026, I am also a Research Scholar at the Oden Institute for Computational Engineering and Sciences at The University of Texas at Austin. My current work is on arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian discontinuous Galerkin methods for moving-boundary problems. I am also interested in combining data-driven methods with high-fidelity simulation; my MSc focused on physics-informed neural networks for computational fluid dynamics.

Research

More on my research →
01

Moving-boundary flows with ALE-DG on GPUs

High-order arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian discontinuous Galerkin methods for flows around moving and deforming bodies.

 02

Galerkin-Boltzmann formulations for nearly incompressible flows

Kinetic-theory based hyperbolic models of nearly incompressible flow, from laminar to turbulent regimes.

 03

Scientific machine learning for fluid dynamics

Physics-informed neural networks and hybrid reduced-order models combined with high-fidelity solvers.
